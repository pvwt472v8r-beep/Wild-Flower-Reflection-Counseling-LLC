Who do you work with?+

Christine offers individual counseling for people ages 16 and older. She also offers professional supervision for Licensed Professional Counselor Candidates (LPCCs).

What is your counseling approach?+

Christine draws on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Person-Centered Therapy, and Solution-Focused Brief Therapy. She is also trained in trauma-informed care and is Safe Zone Certified.

How can I ask about availability and fees?+

Visit Christine's Headway or Psychology Today profile to explore the details listed there and use the contact options provided. Please confirm current availability, session format, fees, and any insurance coverage directly before scheduling.

How do I inquire about LPCC supervision?+

Use the contact options on Christine's Psychology Today profile and mention that your inquiry is about LPCC supervision. Confirm availability, fees, and whether the arrangement meets your licensing requirements directly with Christine.
