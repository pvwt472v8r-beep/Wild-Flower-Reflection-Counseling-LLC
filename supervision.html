Who offers supervision?+

Christine Kayga holds LPC and LPC-S credentials and offers supervision for Licensed Professional Counselor Candidates.

What should I ask before beginning?+

Discuss your licensing requirements, professional goals, supervision format, fees, and current availability with Christine. Confirm that the proposed arrangement meets your licensing board's requirements before beginning.

How do I inquire?+

Use the contact options on Christine's Psychology Today profile and mention LPCC supervision. The supervision inquiry section provides the link.
