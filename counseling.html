A THOUGHTFUL APPROACH

Counseling that starts with you.

Christine's work draws on three approaches, with training in trauma-informed care and Safe Zone certification.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

CBT explores connections between thoughts, feelings, and behaviors, and ways to work with patterns you want to change.

02 / CONNECT

Person-Centered Therapy

A collaborative approach that values your perspective and makes space for your experiences, choices, and goals.

03 / LOOK AHEAD

Solution-Focused Brief Therapy

A focus on your strengths, what is already helping, and practical steps toward the changes that matter to you.
